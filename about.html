About

Built around the idea that recovery needs room to work.

NextWave Wellness sits on the lake, away from the pace of a typical medical office — a private setting for chiropractic, functional medicine, and longevity care under one roof.

Our Philosophy

One plan of care, not three separate ones.

Most people end up piecing their care together — a chiropractor here, a hormone specialist there, a separate recovery studio across town, none of them talking to each other. NextWave was built to close that gap: one intake, one biomarker history, one care team coordinating chiropractic, functional medicine, and longevity therapies around a single plan.

That only works with real time and real space — which is why appointments here aren't run on a fifteen-minute clock, and why we chose a setting on the water instead of a strip-mall suite.
