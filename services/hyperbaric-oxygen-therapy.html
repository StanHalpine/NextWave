How hyperbaric oxygen therapy works.

A hyperbaric oxygen chamber is a sealed chamber where a person breathes nearly 100% oxygen while the air pressure is increased to about 2–3 times normal atmospheric pressure. The higher pressure allows much more oxygen to dissolve into the blood plasma, enabling oxygen to reach tissues that may have poor blood flow. For people interested in healthy aging and longevity, hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) is being explored as a way to support the body's natural repair processes.

Potential longevity benefits being studied

  • Enhanced cellular repair by increasing oxygen delivery to tissues.
  • Reduced inflammation, which is associated with many age-related diseases.
  • Improved circulation through the stimulation of new blood vessel growth in some tissues.
  • Support for cognitive health, with some studies suggesting improvements in memory and brain function in certain populations.
  • Healthier skin and wound healing by promoting collagen production and tissue regeneration.
  • Potential effects on biological aging, with early research suggesting HBOT may influence markers associated with cellular aging, such as telomere length and the removal of senescent ("aging") cells.
