How red light therapy works.

Full-body red light therapy (RLT) uses low levels of red and near-infrared light to penetrate the skin and underlying tissues. These wavelengths are absorbed by the mitochondria — the "powerhouses" of cells — which may help improve cellular energy production (ATP), reduce oxidative stress, and support the body's natural repair processes. Unlike tanning beds, red light therapy does not use ultraviolet (UV) light and does not tan or burn the skin.

Who may benefit most

People interested in healthy aging, recovery, and overall wellness may benefit from full-body red light therapy, including those looking to:

  • Support healthy skin by promoting collagen production, improving skin tone, and reducing the appearance of fine lines.
  • Enhance muscle recovery and reduce post-exercise soreness after workouts.
  • Ease joint discomfort and stiffness, particularly in people with mild musculoskeletal pain.
  • Reduce inflammation, which plays a role in many age-related health conditions.
  • Support circulation and tissue repair by increasing blood flow to treated areas.
  • Promote healthy aging, as emerging research suggests red and near-infrared light may help support mitochondrial function and cellular resilience.
