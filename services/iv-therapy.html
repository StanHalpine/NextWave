How IV therapy works.

IV drips deliver fluids, vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and other nutrients directly into the bloodstream through an intravenous infusion. By bypassing the digestive system, IV therapy can provide rapid hydration and ensure nutrients are immediately available for the body to use. For health-conscious individuals, IV therapy is often used as a targeted wellness tool to support recovery, optimize nutrient levels, and complement a healthy lifestyle.

Who may benefit most

People who may benefit from IV drips include those who:

  • Want to support hydration and recovery after intense exercise, travel, illness, or periods of stress.
  • Have increased nutrient demands due to active lifestyles, aging, or high physical output.
  • Have difficulty maintaining optimal nutrient levels through diet alone.
  • Are seeking support for energy, immune function, and overall wellness.
  • Want to complement a longevity-focused routine that includes exercise, nutrition, sleep, and preventive health strategies.

Common wellness-focused benefits

Common wellness-focused IV drip benefits may include:

  • Improved hydration to support energy, circulation, and cellular function.
  • Nutrient replenishment to support normal metabolic processes.
  • Antioxidant support to help manage oxidative stress.
  • Recovery support after physical exertion or periods of increased demand.
  • Support for healthy aging by helping maintain optimal levels of key nutrients involved in cellular health.
