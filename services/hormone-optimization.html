How hormone optimization works.

The DUTCH (Dried Urine Test for Comprehensive Hormones) test provides a detailed assessment of hormone levels and how the body metabolizes them. It measures key hormones such as estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, cortisol, DHEA, and their metabolites, offering insight into hormone production, balance, and stress response. This information can help healthcare providers identify hormonal imbalances and develop more personalized treatment plans.

Why consider bioidentical hormone replacement?

Bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT) uses hormones that are chemically identical to those naturally produced by the body. When prescribed and monitored by a qualified healthcare provider, BHRT may help restore hormone balance and relieve symptoms related to hormone deficiency or imbalance. Treatment should always be individualized based on a patient's symptoms, medical history, and laboratory findings.

Common symptoms of hormone imbalance

Women may experience

  • Fatigue or low energy
  • Mood swings, anxiety, or depression
  • Hot flashes and night sweats
  • Weight gain or difficulty losing weight
  • Irregular or painful menstrual cycles
  • Low libido
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Brain fog or difficulty concentrating
  • Vaginal dryness
  • Hair thinning or hair loss

Men may experience

  • Fatigue or decreased stamina
  • Reduced libido
  • Erectile dysfunction
  • Loss of muscle mass and strength
  • Increased body fat, especially around the abdomen
  • Mood changes, irritability, or depression
  • Difficulty concentrating or memory problems
  • Poor sleep
  • Decreased motivation
  • Hair thinning or reduced body hair

Because many of these symptoms can also be caused by other medical conditions, hormone testing and evaluation by a qualified healthcare provider can help determine whether a hormonal imbalance is contributing to the symptoms and guide appropriate treatment.
