Included markers

  • HbA1C — Measures average blood sugar levels over the past 2–3 months and helps assess metabolic health and diabetes risk.
  • Fasting Insulin — Evaluates how effectively the body regulates insulin and can provide insight into insulin sensitivity and metabolic function.
  • Omega 3:6 Ratio — Measures the balance of essential fatty acids to assess inflammation regulation, cardiovascular health, and cellular function.

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) — Provides insight into overall health by measuring blood cells related to oxygen delivery, immunity, and inflammation.
  • Complete Metabolic Panel (CMP) — Assesses organ function, electrolyte balance, blood sugar regulation, and markers of metabolic health.
  • Lipid Levels — Measures cholesterol and triglyceride levels to evaluate cardiovascular risk and metabolic wellness.
  • CRP (C-Reactive Protein) — Measures systemic inflammation levels, which can provide insight into cardiovascular risk and overall health status.

Total Toxin Testing

$900 walk-in

Members save 10%.

Utilizing Vibrant Wellness, Total Toxin Testing provides insight into a person's exposure to environmental compounds such as mold and mycotoxins, heavy metals, pesticides, herbicides, and plastic-related chemicals. Understanding an individual's toxic burden can help identify potential environmental stressors that may be impacting overall health and provide valuable information for creating a more personalized wellness plan.

Chronic exposure to certain toxins may contribute to increased oxidative stress, inflammation, impaired detoxification pathways, hormone disruption, immune system stress, and changes in cellular function. Over time, a higher toxic burden may affect energy levels, cognitive function, metabolic health, recovery, and overall resilience.

Identifying potential toxin exposure can help

  • Create awareness of environmental factors that may be affecting health.
  • Guide targeted lifestyle, nutrition, and environmental changes.
  • Support the body's natural detoxification processes.
  • Reduce unnecessary exposure and promote long-term wellness.

By understanding the body's toxic load, individuals can take a more proactive approach to protecting cellular health, improving vitality, and supporting healthy aging.

Gut Health

$900 walk-in

Includes 1 food sensitivity test, 2 at-home stool sample tests, and a 30 minute consult. Members save 10%.

Gut health testing provides insight into how the digestive system is functioning by evaluating factors such as food sensitivities, gut inflammation, digestion, intestinal permeability, and the balance of beneficial and potentially harmful microorganisms within the gut microbiome. Understanding these findings can help identify areas where personalized nutrition and lifestyle strategies may improve overall health.

A healthy gut plays a critical role in immune function, nutrient absorption, metabolism, hormone regulation, and communication between the gut and brain. When the intestinal barrier becomes compromised ("leaky gut") or the microbiome becomes imbalanced (dysbiosis), it may contribute to increased inflammation, digestive discomfort, immune system activation, and challenges with overall wellness.

Gut testing can help

  • Identify foods that may be contributing to unwanted reactions or inflammation.
  • Better understand digestive function and nutrient absorption.
  • Assess microbiome balance and support a healthier gut environment.
  • Develop more personalized nutrition and lifestyle strategies.
  • Support energy, immune resilience, metabolic health, and overall well-being.

By understanding the unique signals from their gut, individuals can take a more proactive approach to improving digestion, reducing unnecessary stress on the body, and supporting long-term health and healthy aging.

Food Sensitivity

$350 walk-in

Members save 25%.

Food sensitivity testing provides insight into how the body may be reacting to specific foods by identifying immune responses that may be associated with certain dietary triggers. Understanding these findings can help individuals make more informed nutrition choices and develop a personalized approach to supporting digestion, inflammation management, and overall wellness.

Identifying potential food sensitivities may help people better understand connections between their diet and symptoms such as digestive discomfort, bloating, fatigue, skin concerns, headaches, or difficulty achieving optimal health goals. The results can serve as a guide for targeted dietary adjustments rather than relying on generalized nutrition recommendations.

Who may benefit

People who may benefit from food sensitivity testing include those who:

  • Experience ongoing digestive issues such as bloating, irregular bowel patterns, or discomfort after eating.
  • Have unexplained fatigue, brain fog, or low energy despite healthy lifestyle habits.
  • Struggle with inflammation, skin concerns, or recurring wellness challenges.
  • Are working toward body composition, performance, or longevity goals and want a more personalized nutrition strategy.
  • Have tried multiple diets or approaches without understanding which foods best support their individual needs.

By identifying potential food-related triggers, individuals can make more informed decisions about their nutrition, reduce unnecessary stress on the body, and create a dietary strategy that supports long-term health and well-being.

Galleri Cancer Test

$1,050 walk-in

Members save 10%.

The Galleri® cancer screening test is a multi-cancer early detection blood test that looks for specific patterns of DNA changes associated with cancer. It is designed to detect signals associated with over 50 types of cancer, including many cancers that currently do not have recommended routine screening options. By identifying potential cancer signals earlier — sometimes before symptoms appear — the test may help individuals and their healthcare providers pursue timely follow-up evaluation.

Learning these findings can provide valuable insight for individuals focused on proactive health management, prevention, and longevity. Early detection is one of the most important factors in improving cancer outcomes, and understanding personal cancer risk can help support more informed health decisions.

People who may benefit

  • Adults at higher risk for cancer due to age, family history, lifestyle factors, or environmental exposures.
  • Individuals who want a more proactive approach to health screening beyond traditional preventive care.
  • Those focused on longevity and health optimization who want additional tools to monitor and protect long-term health.
  • People who want greater awareness of potential health risks and a more personalized approach to prevention.

The Galleri test is designed to complement — not replace — recommended cancer screenings such as mammograms, colonoscopies, Pap tests, and other routine evaluations. A positive result does not diagnose cancer but identifies a signal that may warrant further diagnostic evaluation with a healthcare provider.
