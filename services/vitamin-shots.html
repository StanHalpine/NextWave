How vitamin shots work.

Vitamin shots deliver nutrients directly into the muscle, where they are gradually absorbed into the bloodstream. This bypasses the digestive system, making them a convenient option for people who have difficulty absorbing certain vitamins or who have increased nutritional needs.

Who may benefit most

People who may benefit from vitamin injections include those who:

  • Have diagnosed vitamin deficiencies.
  • Have digestive conditions that impair nutrient absorption.
  • Experience fatigue related to certain nutrient deficiencies.
  • Are looking to support energy metabolism, immune health, or overall wellness under medical guidance.
  • Want a convenient way to maintain specific nutrient levels when oral supplements are not ideal.

Glutathione

$45

What it is

A powerful antioxidant molecule that supports the body's natural detoxification processes and helps protect cells from oxidative stress.

How it works

Glutathione is one of your body's primary defense systems against free radicals and cellular damage. A single injection delivers a concentrated dose directly into your bloodstream, bypassing digestion to ensure maximum availability where your body needs it most.

Why it matters

Who benefits from this shot

  • People with high oxidative stress from demanding exercise, travel, or chronic stress.
  • Those focused on skin health and anti-aging — glutathione supports cellular renewal.
  • Anyone seeking comprehensive antioxidant and detoxification support.
  • Individuals recovering from illness or intensive physical performance.

Your body makes glutathione naturally, but levels decline with age and stress. A shot restores levels when you need recovery support most.

NAD (25 mg)

$45

What it is

NAD (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme essential for cellular energy production, DNA repair, and metabolic function.

How it works

NAD levels naturally decline with age, contributing to fatigue, slower recovery, and reduced cellular function. A direct injection restores NAD levels, supporting your cells' ability to produce energy and repair themselves — effects you typically feel within hours.

Why it matters

Who benefits from this shot

  • Athletes and active people seeking faster recovery and enhanced performance.
  • Those experiencing age-related fatigue or flagging energy levels.
  • People focused on longevity and supporting healthy cellular aging.
  • Anyone recovering from illness or intensive mental or physical demands.

NAD drives the energy factories in your cells. When they're running well, you feel it — sharper, faster, more endurance.

Vitamin B12

What it is

Vitamin B12 (methylcobalamin) is an essential nutrient for energy metabolism, red blood cell formation, and healthy nerve function.

How it works

Many people have suboptimal B12 levels — especially those on plant-based diets, with digestive issues, or over age 50. A direct injection bypasses absorption barriers and delivers B12 straight to your cells where it's needed for energy production and neurological health.

Why it matters

Who benefits from this shot

  • People with fatigue that hasn't improved with other interventions — B12 deficiency is often overlooked.
  • Plant-based eaters and those with digestive absorption issues.
  • Adults over 50, who naturally absorb less B12 from food.
  • Anyone seeking sharp mental clarity and consistent energy throughout the day.

Low B12 feels like invisible fatigue. A lot of energy crashes that people blame on sleep actually come from insufficient B12 — fixing it is often a revelation.

Vitamin D3 (12,500 IU)

$45

What it is

Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) is a hormone-like nutrient essential for bone health, immune function, muscle strength, and mood regulation.

How it works

Most people are vitamin D deficient, especially in winter or if they spend limited time outdoors. A D3 injection delivers a direct dose that your body can use immediately to support calcium absorption, immune response, and healthy inflammatory balance.

Why it matters

Who benefits from this shot

  • Anyone with low vitamin D levels (confirmed by testing) or who lives in low-sun climates.
  • People with seasonal mood changes or persistent fatigue linked to D deficiency.
  • Those seeking to optimize bone density, immunity, and muscle function.
  • Athletes and active people who need strong immune and musculoskeletal health.

Vitamin D deficiency is silent and widespread. If you feel seasonally flat or your bones need support, D3 often makes an immediate difference.
