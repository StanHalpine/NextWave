# Text messages — for the A2P campaign form

> Generated by `npm run sms:templates` from `server/src/integrations/templates.ts`. **Do not edit by hand** —
> change the wording in that file and regenerate. A test fails if this copy is out of date.

Every text may contain only the patient's **first name**, the appointment **day** and **time** — never a
service, provider, room or any clinical detail. Texts are sent only between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. clinic time.

## Opt-in

A checkbox on the public booking page, **unchecked by default**, immediately above the "Request appointment" button:

> ☐ Text me appointment confirmations and reminders. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

The time it was ticked and the exact words shown are stored with the booking. A front-desk team member can also
record consent given verbally or on paper on the patient's profile; how it was given is kept in the audit log.

## Keywords the system answers

Matched against the whole message, ignoring case and surrounding punctuation.

| Keyword | Action |
|---|---|
| STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT | Texts to that number stop; the patient is told once |
| START, UNSTOP | Texts may resume (consent already on file) |
| HELP, INFO | Replies with the clinic's phone number |
| C | Marks the patient's next appointment as confirmed by them |

## Messages we send

### `appt_confirmation_v2`

**When:** When an appointment is confirmed, to a patient who consented to texts

**Text (exactly as stored):**

    NextWave Wellness: Hi {first}, you're confirmed for {day} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or call us to change. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Example:** NextWave Wellness: Hi Ingrid, you're confirmed for Saturday, Oct 10 at 9:00 AM. Reply C to confirm or call us to change. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Size:** 143 characters · GSM-7 · 1 segment — longest realistic: 168 characters · 2 segments

### `appt_reminder_24h_v2`

**When:** The day before the appointment (within 24 hours of it), to a patient who consented to texts

**Text (exactly as stored):**

    NextWave Wellness: Reminder — {first}, see you tomorrow at {time}. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Example:** NextWave Wellness: Reminder — Ingrid, see you tomorrow at 9:00 AM. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Size:** 89 characters · UCS-2 · 2 segments — longest realistic: 113 characters · 2 segments

> ⚠ Contains `—`, which is not in the standard SMS (GSM-7) alphabet. That makes the **whole message Unicode**: 70 characters per segment instead of 160, so it costs more segments. Replacing it with a plain hyphen (`-`) keeps it GSM-7.
